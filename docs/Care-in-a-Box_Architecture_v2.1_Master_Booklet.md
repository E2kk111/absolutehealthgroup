## 1. Executive Summary

**One institutional care platform. Multiple applications. One governed execution layer. One measurable economic engine.**

### The market buys Care-in-a-Box™
Absolute Health Group packages specialty access, recovery operations, wound intelligence, care navigation, implementation, measurement and evidence into deployable institutional programs.

### The machine meters governed work
GitHealth™ converts AI activity into Workflow Units™ with defined evidence, authority, completion criteria, Cost-to-Goal™ and ROI. Tokens remain COGS telemetry — not the product.

### AION™ provides intelligence + authority
Clinical, operational and financial intelligence operate under human authority, PolicyPulse™ regulatory awareness and RegOS™ versioned execution controls.

Applications include Recovery, ACCESS, CJR-X, Burn, Wound, Rural, Senior, Regenerative/Longevity, Hydration & Recovery and Specialty Care.

## 3. The Four Rails

1. **Care Delivery** — assessments, care planning, caregiver engagement, monitoring, transitions and specialist access.
2. **Coverage + Coding** — eligibility, NCD/LCD, billing articles, CPT/HCPCS, ICD-10, modifiers and documentation.
3. **CMS / Value-Based Payment** — covered services when eligible, PIN/CHI/TCM/RPM/RTM/APCM, ACCESS, CJR-X, ACO/VBC and payer contracts.
4. **Proof + Economics** — claim/model evidence, remittance, outcomes, total cost and Cost-to-Goal™.

> **A code is not coverage. Coverage is not payment. Payment is not proof. GitHealth connects all four.**

## 16. Trust, Governance & Deployment

- **CMS-Aligned** — map workflows to applicable CMS coverage, coding, model and billing rules. Do not imply CMS endorsement.
- **FDA-Aware** — validate product/device status and intended use where applicable. Do not imply broad platform FDA approval.
- **Human Authority** — consequential clinical, payment and PHI actions remain within defined authority and approval controls.
- **Evidence First** — preserve source, rule version, authority, action, outcome and payment evidence.
