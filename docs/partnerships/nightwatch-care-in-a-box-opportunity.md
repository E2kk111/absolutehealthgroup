## Executive decision

**Proceed to nonexclusive partnership diligence, not deployment.** Test one assisted-living or CCRC nighttime-observation workflow only after device safety, privacy, security, regulatory applicability, interoperability, liability, and clinical governance have been evaluated. Do not purchase robot inventory or promise staffing reductions based on interviews.

## Source-derived market evidence (interviews, not independently verified results)

- Ten conversations: eight operator-side and two market-expert conversations. Five operator-side conversations were CCRC-focused, per the deck.
- Operators repeatedly valued a deployment without devices/cameras inside resident rooms. Consent, privacy, sensor maintenance, and installation burden were recurring objections to room-based systems.
- Two operators independently suggested **$2,000–$3,000/month** as a pricing range. This is stated willingness to pay, not contracted revenue.
- One operator estimated **30–40 minutes per shift** could be freed. No prospective time-and-motion study was supplied.
- Operators expressed interest in pilots, including potential paths via Life Care Services, Asbury, RiverSpring, and Traditions. These are **not** signed pilots or AHG relationships.
- The deck reports operator anecdotes about falls, litigation, documentation accuracy, and labor. These are **not** independently validated outcome or financial benchmarks.
- The deck's slide 6 contains internally inconsistent arithmetic (for example, $80K − $48K is $32K, not $43K). Do not reuse its claimed bottom-line totals without reconciling assumptions.

## Pilot: one site, one nighttime workflow, 90 days

1. **Days 1–30 — qualification:** Select a willing assisted-living/CCRC unit (approximately 40–60 residents only as a planning assumption); review intended use, FDA applicability, applicable electrical/robotic safety requirements, resident privacy/consent, data hosting, data access, security, insurance, and nurse-call/handheld integration. Agree on baseline definitions.
2. **Days 31–60 — shadow-mode observation:** Only after approval, test nonclinical alerts in parallel with existing rounds, staffing and escalation. Measure accuracy, uptime, staff burden, and resident disruption. No autonomous clinical action or reduction in mandated rounds.
3. **Days 61–90 — evaluation:** Reconcile baseline vs observed performance; record false/missed alerts, time to acknowledgement and resolution, workflow completion, downtime, cost per governed completed check, staff feedback, and safety incidents. Joint go/no-go review.

### Decision gates before any resident-facing deployment

- Written intended-use and regulatory analysis (FDA status cannot be inferred from the deck); relevant UL/electrical and robotic safety testing.
- Privacy impact assessment, resident/family consent and notice as applicable, data flow and retention, HIPAA applicability, vendor contracts, US/onshore hosting claims independently verified, breach allocation.
- Working alert integration to existing staff devices/call systems; failure and cellular fallback demonstrated.
- Carpet/threshold/clutter navigation, charging and backup, two-bed rooms, CPAP/interference, resident wandering, robot-as-trip-hazard, staff privacy.
- Defined minimum uptime, failure reporting, response times, service coverage, liability allocation, and incident escalation.
- Facility clinical/operational approval of any workflow change and baseline metric definitions.

## Pilot scorecard (targets to negotiate; no achieved metrics)

| Domain | Baseline / measurement |
| --- | --- |
| Rounding completion | Existing manual check completion, corroborated by independent observation |
| Alert quality | False-positive and false-negative rates against adjudicated observations |
| Response | Alert acknowledgement, escalation, and resolution times |
| Reliability | Operating uptime, lost connectivity, charge interruptions, recovery |
| Staff burden | Minutes per shift and exception-handling workload |
| Resident experience | Nighttime disruptions, privacy complaints, safety events |
| Economics | Actual vendor cost + AHG cost + facility effort; cost per completed governed workflow |

**Success cannot be defined solely as staff reduction or claimed fall prevention.** Any claims of improved outcomes, fewer falls, lower liability, savings, or ROI require appropriately designed measurement and review.
